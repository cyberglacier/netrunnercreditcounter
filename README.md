Netrunner Credit Counter
Simple counter to keep track of your credits in the LCG Netrunner.
When you press the symbol in the middle, it opens up a settings menu to switch to light mode and to reset your credits.
This tracker is neither asscoiated with Wizards of the Coast, Fantasy Flight Games nor Null Signal Games.

You can find and download the APK under: https://github.com/glaziola/netrunnercreditcounter/tree/master/app
